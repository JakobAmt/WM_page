Important information regarding the use of WatchMate watch holders.

Intended Use

WatchMate watch holders are designed for the stationary display of wristwatches on a stable, level surface.

WatchMate products are intended for indoor use and are not designed for carrying or transporting watches, supporting multiple watches, or use as toys.

Before Use

Always place the WatchMate on a stable, level and suitable surface before use.

Ensure that all components are properly assembled and securely connected before placing a watch on the holder.

After placing your watch on the WatchMate, check that the holder remains stable and that the watch is securely supported.

Suitability

Watches vary in size, weight and design. The user is responsible for ensuring that the WatchMate is suitable for their particular watch and intended use.

For valuable or particularly heavy watches, extra care should be taken to ensure that the holder is stable and that the watch is securely supported.

Condition of Product

Do not use a WatchMate if it is cracked, damaged, deformed or otherwise appears unsafe.

Inspect the product regularly and discontinue use if its condition has deteriorated.

Do not modify or alter the product, as modifications may affect its stability or intended function.

Product Variations

WatchMate products are 3D printed. Minor variations in surface finish, layer lines, colour and dimensions may occur between individual products. These are normal characteristics of the manufacturing process.

Damage and Liability

WatchMate is designed for normal watch display use. However, no product can guarantee that accidental damage will never occur.

Users are responsible for following the provided assembly, safety and usage instructions and for ensuring that the product is properly assembled, stable and suitable for their watch.
